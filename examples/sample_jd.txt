Senior Data Analyst - Lending Analytics
FinServe Technologies, Bengaluru (Hybrid)

About the role
We are looking for a Data Analyst to own reporting and insights for our lending business.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain self-serve dashboards in Power BI for business stakeholders
- Write complex SQL to analyse loan disbursal, collections and credit risk data
- Automate ETL pipelines using Python and Airflow on AWS
- Design and analyse A/B tests and communicate insights to leadership
- Ensure data quality through automated validation checks

What we're looking for
- 1-3 years of experience in analytics, ideally in fintech or lending
- Strong SQL and Python (Pandas)
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Familiarity with AWS (S3, Athena, Redshift) and dbt
- Excellent stakeholder management and storytelling skills
